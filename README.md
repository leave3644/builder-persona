## English

### Why This Playbook?

Most "personal branding" advice tells you to pick a niche, write hooks, and post daily. That produces content that sounds like a content agency, not a builder.

This playbook takes a different approach:

| Problem | Our Approach |
|:--------|:------------|
| Generic "founder content" | Extract real thinking from real building experience |
| Same hooks everywhere | Platform-native voice — LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and XHS are different personas |
| "Post every day" grind | Depth > frequency. Each post carries a distinct insight. |
| Content-first strategy | Build-first. Content is a byproduct of the work. |

### Phase 1: Persona Definition

Your content angle is not "what you know." It's **how you see the same thing differently because of what you're building.**

**The Formula:**
> "While everyone focuses on X, I pay attention to Y because I see Z from building this."

**Intake Questions** (ask these, not marketing questions):

| Category | Questions |
|:---------|:---------|
| **Product & Reality** | What are you building right now? What do you see daily that outsiders don't? |
| **Decision & Judgment** | What decisions are actually hard? What separates good vs average in your domain? |
| **Process & Behavior** | What does "good work" look like step-by-step? Where does effort get hidden? |
| **Contrarian** | What do you believe that your space would disagree with? What's over-indexed? |
| **Personal Shift** | What changed your mind recently? What did you used to believe? |

**Never ask directly**: "What content do you want to make?" / "Who is your audience?" / "What's your niche?" — derive these from the answers above.

---

### Phase 2: Content Pillars

| Pillar | Focus | Example |
|:-------|:------|:--------|
| **Process > Outcome** | Making invisible work visible | How people think, decide, execute — not the result |
| **AI-Native vs Non-AI-Native** | Behavioral difference, not tools | Why tools alone don't create leverage |
| **Building in Public** | Real, not performative | What surprised you, what changed your thinking |
| **Growth as Systems** | Underlying mechanics | Distribution loops, not tactics |

**Rotation**: Reflection → Industry observation → Product-linked insight → Growth/system thinking → Repeat

**Mix**: 60-70% thinking/insight, 20-30% product-adjacent, minimal direct promotion

---

### Phase 3: Voice Calibration

| Axis | Position |
|:-----|:---------|
| Emotional vs Analytical | Balanced, slightly analytical |
| Loud vs Quiet | Quiet confidence |
| Abstract vs Concrete | Concrete first, insight after |
| Teaching vs Observing | Observing |

**Red Lines — Content Must NEVER:**
- Use "Not X, but Y" constructions
- Sound like overly packaged insights
- Use "In a world where..." openers
- Use list-style hooks ("3 reasons why...")
- Use over-compressed punchlines ("AI won't replace X. X using AI will.")
- Try to sound inspirational or use engagement bait

### Phase 4: Platform Playbooks

#### Platform Persona Matrix

| Dimension | LinkedIn | Twitter/X | XHS |
|:----------|:---------|:----------|:----|
| Language | English | English (some Chinese) | Chinese |
| Persona | Professional narrator | Social node | Real self |
| Subject | "Me + Industry" | "My circle" | "Me" |
| Vulnerability | Low (only resolved struggles) | Zero | High |
| Core action | Long-form articles | Retweet + one-liner | Vent + recommend |
| Engagement style | Open-ended question close | Like/retweet/congrats | Ask for help + share + vent |

#### LinkedIn

- Lead with insight, not hook tricks
- Paragraph-based storytelling, full paragraphs only
- Pattern: **Personal anecdote opener → Authority + practice → Open-ended close**
- Real experience posts > AI analysis > Industry news

#### Twitter/X — Algorithm & Engagement Rules

**Scoring (from open-sourced algorithm):**

| Action | Points |
|:-------|:-------|
| Reply to your own tweet's comments | +75 pts |
| Reply to other accounts | +13.5 pts |
| Someone reports you | -369 pts |
| "Not interested" flag | -74 pts |

**6 Ranking Systems**: Graph Jet (relationship networks), Sim Clusters (niche down!), TwHin (action history), Real Graph (engagement prediction), Tweep Cred (reputation score), Trust & Safety

**Daily routine**: 3 private lists (10 bigger / 10 same-sized / 10 smaller accounts) → 15 min/day adding value through comments → reply to every mention

**Reach suppressors**: External links, being reported, mixed-topic posting, low recent engagement

#### XHS (Xiaohongshu)

- **Dual-title strategy**: Cover title = hook for users (痛点/爽点/痒点), Body title = keywords for algorithm (主题+长尾关键词)
- Vulnerability OK, complaints OK, self-deprecating humor works
- Broken lines, rhythm-based, pause-based reading structure

### Phase 5: Hook Bank

**6 Types That Work:**

| Type | Example |
|:-----|:--------|
| "I tried..." (experiment) | "I tried a few AI investing apps recently." |
| "When I first..." (relatability) | "When I first looked into AI investing, I thought it would just tell me what to buy." |
| "If you're looking for..." (search-intent) | "If you're looking for an AI investing app in 2025, most lists will give you the same names." |
| "Most tools/people..." (soft contrarian) | "Most AI investing tools look similar at first." |
| "The real difference..." (reframing) | "The real gap is not access to tools, but how you use them." |
| "What I found..." (conclusion-led) | "What I found is most tools either overwhelm you or oversimplify." |

**10 Anti-Patterns — NEVER use:**

| # | Type | Example |
|:--|:-----|:--------|
| 1 | Over-dramatic hype | "This AI tool will change how you invest forever" |
| 2 | Fake authority / guru | "Here's how you should invest using AI" |
| 3 | Generic viral cliches | "No one is talking about this..." |
| 4 | Hard "not X, but Y" | "It's not about X, it's about Y" |
| 5 | List-style | "3 reasons why AI investing is better" |
| 6 | Over-abstract | "In a world where AI reshapes everything..." |
| 7 | Over-compressed punchlines | "AI won't replace investors. Investors using AI will." |
| 8 | Aggressive contrarian | "You're doing this wrong" |
| 9 | Marketing copy | "Unlock the power of AI investing" |
| 10 | No lived experience | Claims not backed by actual usage |

### Phase 6: Cross-Platform Repurposing

**Core Rule: Same idea, different entry point. Never reuse hooks.**

| Platform | Abstraction | Tone | Structure | CTA |
|:---------|:-----------|:-----|:----------|:----|
| LinkedIn | Idea-first, highest | Composed, thoughtful | Full sentences, structured flow | "Curious how others think about this" |
| Twitter/X | Punchline-first, lowest | Detached, ironic | Fragments, almost no grammar | None — just drop thought |
| XHS | Story-first, mid | Emotional, controlled | Broken lines, rhythm-based | "你会懂这种感觉吗" |

**Example — same core idea:**

> Skills are hard to verify in AI era

- **LinkedIn**: "Resumes are becoming the new NFTs" → conceptual, category framing
- **Twitter/X**: "resumes are the new NFTs" → stripped, punchline only
- **XHS**: "我付钱买过一个完全用不了的设计" → personal story entry

**Workflow**: LinkedIn first (highest depth) → Compress to Twitter/X → Adapt to XHS (Chinese, story-first)

---

### Phase 7: Growth Tactics

**Draft review rules:**
1. Push for shorter, sharper, more intentional — compression > explanation
2. Correct positioning, not just wording — fix thinking direction
3. Push for non-obvious angles — unexpected metaphors, "pause and reread" lines
4. Optimize for category narrative, not just messaging

大部分"个人品牌"建议都在教你：选赛道、写钩子、每天发帖。这样产出的内容像内容代理商写的，不像一个 Builder 写的。

这个 Playbook 的方法不同：

| 问题 | 我们的方法 |
|:-----|:---------|
| 千篇一律的"创始人内容" | 从真实的构建经验中提取真正的思考 |
| 各平台同一个钩子 | 平台原生语感——LinkedIn、Twitter/X、小红书是三个不同的人格 |
| "每天发帖"的内卷 | 深度 > 频率。每条帖子承载一个独立洞察。 |
| 内容先行策略 | 构建先行。内容是工作的副产品。 |

### 内容角度公式

> "当所有人都在关注 X 的时候，我关注的是 Y，因为我在构建的过程中看到了 Z。"

### 拒绝的角度：
- "AI 好强大" 类型的角度
- 以工具为中心的叙事
- 教育者/讲解者定位
- 任何听起来是"内容先行"而非"构建先行"的东西

### 4 大内容支柱

| 支柱 | 关注点 |
|:-----|:------|
| **过程 > 结果** | 让隐形工作可见。人们怎么思考、决策、执行。 |
| **AI原生 vs 非AI原生** | 行为差异，不是工具差异。为什么工具本身不创造杠杆。 |
| **真实的 Build in Public** | 什么让你惊讶，什么挑战了你的假设，什么改变了你的思考。不是假透明。 |
| **增长即系统** | 底层机制。自动化 vs 真正的杠杆。分发循环。 |

**轮换规则**：反思 → 行业观察 → 产品相关洞察 → 增长/系统思维 → 循环

**配比**：60-70% 思考/洞察，20-30% 产品相关，极少直接推广

### 三平台人格矩阵

| 维度 | LinkedIn | Twitter/X | 小红书 |
|:-----|:---------|:----------|:------|
| 语言 | 英文 | 英文为主 | 中文 |
| 人格 | 专业叙事者 | 社交节点 | 真实自我 |
| 主角 | "我+行业" | "我的圈子" | "我自己" |
| 脆弱度 | 低（只展示克服后的故事） | 零 | 高 |
| 核心动作 | 写长文 | 转发+一句话 | 吐槽+推荐 |
| 互动风格 | 开放式提问收尾 | 点赞转发congrats | 求助+分享+发牢骚 |

### 小红书专属策略

**双标题策略**：
- **封面标题**（吸引用户）：痛点 / 爽点 / 痒点
- **正文标题**（喂算法）：主题 + 长尾关键词

**语气**：可以脆弱、可以吐槽、可以自嘲（"本人幼儿园级别投流选手"）

**结构**：断行、节奏感、留白式阅读。不是 LinkedIn 的段落结构。

### Twitter/X 算法与互动规则

**评分系统（来自开源算法）：**

| 行为 | 分数 |
|:-----|:-----|
| 回复自己推文的评论 | +75 分 |
| 回复其他账号 | +13.5 分 |
| 被举报 | -369 分 |
| 被标记"不感兴趣" | -74 分 |

**每日互动流程**：
1. 建立 3 个私密列表，每个 10 个账号（大号 / 同级号 / 小号）
2. 每天花 15 分钟浏览列表，通过有价值的评论互动
3. 进入通知 → 提及 → 回复每一条评论
4. 目标：触发回复链（你回复 → 他们回复 → 算法识别为持续对话）

### 跨平台复用规则

**核心规则：同一想法，不同切入点。永远不要跨平台复用钩子。**

| 平台 | 抽象层级 | 语气 | 句式 | CTA |
|:-----|:--------|:-----|:-----|:----|
| LinkedIn | 观点先行，最高抽象 | 沉稳、深思 | 完整句子，结构化 | "好奇别人怎么看这个" |
| Twitter/X | 金句先行，最低抽象 | 疏离、讽刺 | 碎片化，几乎无语法 | 无——直接丢想法 |
| 小红书 | 故事先行，中等抽象 | 情感化、克制 | 断行、节奏感 | "你会懂这种感觉吗" |

**示例——同一核心想法跨平台：**

> 核心想法：AI 时代技能难以验证

- **LinkedIn**: "Resumes are becoming the new NFTs" → 概念性、品类框架
- **Twitter/X**: "resumes are the new NFTs" → 精简、只留金句
- **小红书**: "我付钱买过一个完全用不了的设计" → 个人故事切入

## Who This Is For

| You are... | This playbook helps you... |
|:-----------|:--------------------------|
| A **solo founder** who can build products but can't write about them | Define your content angle from what you're already building — no "content strategy" needed |
| A **technical builder** who posts once a month and hates it | Get a system that makes posting feel like thinking out loud, not performing |
| A **growth lead** shipping content across EN + CN platforms | Use the 3-platform persona matrix to stop copy-pasting the same post everywhere |
| An **operator** tired of sounding like a content agency | Kill the "Not X, but Y" hooks and write like yourself |

**This is NOT for:**
- Influencers optimizing for virality over substance
- People who want "10 LinkedIn templates" to copy-paste
- Anyone not actively building something

## Real Examples

Posts created using this framework, across three platforms from the same builder:

### LinkedIn — Professional Narrator

> *One reason AI still feels abstract to a lot of people is that most examples are still built for technical audiences.*
>
> *I've been thinking more about what AI agents could look like in everyday family life instead.*
>
> *A retired mom who can turn her travel footage into a polished video without learning editing software. A working dad who gets useful takeaways from the latest cardiovascular research without reading dense medical papers. A grandma who gets gentle reminders to keep up with her rehab movements after hip surgery.*
>
> *That's when AI starts becoming real — not when it sounds impressive, but when it becomes legible in daily life.*

**Why it works**: Concrete first, insight after. No "In a world where AI..." — starts from real family scenarios, lands on a reframe. Observing, not teaching.

---

### Twitter/X — Social Node

> *Everyone talks about AI content.*
>
> *Very few people talk about AI distribution.*
>
> *Content is cheap now. Attention is not.*

**Why it works**: 4 lines. One contrast. No CTA. Drops the thought and walks away. Slightly savage tone without attacking anyone.

---

### XHS (Xiaohongshu) — Real Self

> *十年前，我从亚特兰大搬到旧金山。*
> *不是冲动，是一种"直觉式的迁徙"——*
> *当你隐约知道自己的人生将被重新排列，但还不知道会变成什么样。*
>
> *城市很自由，我却很慌。*
>
> *十年之后，我又回到 Dolores Park。坐在同样的位置。看着同样的阳光。*
> *但我已经不再问那些问题了。*
>
> *人生不是靠抓住每个机会赢的。人生是靠做对的选择赢的。*

**Why it works**: Broken lines, rhythm-based. High vulnerability — LinkedIn Kelly would never write this. Grounded in a specific scene (Dolores Park), not abstract reflection. Emotional but controlled.
